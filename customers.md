# Customers

Customers are the starting point for the sales workflow. Opslora uses customer records when creating orders, generating invoices, and reviewing business activity.

## What you can do

- View the customer list.
- Search or filter customers where available.
- Create a new customer.
- Edit existing customer information.
- Maintain identity, contact, billing, and tax details.

## Create a customer

1. Open Customers.
2. Choose Create customer.
3. Enter the customer name and required contact details.
4. Add billing and tax details when they are needed for invoicing.
5. Save the customer.

## Update a customer

1. Open Customers.
2. Select the customer.
3. Edit the details.
4. Save the changes.

Update customer information before creating orders or invoices so downstream records use the correct details.

## Tips

- Use consistent customer names to avoid duplicates.
- Keep tax and billing fields current before invoicing.
- Check customer details when an invoice looks incorrect.

## Common questions

### Why does an order show an old customer detail?

Orders and invoices may snapshot customer details at the time they are created. Update the customer record first, then create the new downstream record.

### Can I delete customers?

The current application focuses on creating and updating customers. If a customer already has activity, prefer updating the record rather than removing history.
