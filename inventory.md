# Inventory

Inventory is the application workspace for sellable products and stock. It helps users choose the right product when creating orders and understand whether stock needs attention.

This page documents the application experience only. It does not describe any internal inventory service or backend implementation.

## What you can do

- View the product catalogue.
- Create a product.
- Edit product details.
- Review stock balance.
- Adjust stock.
- Use product-backed lines when creating orders.

## Product information

Keep product records accurate so orders and invoices use the right commercial details.

Useful product fields include:

- product name
- SKU or product code
- unit of measure
- sale price
- tax rate
- HSN/SAC or tax classification where applicable

## Stock balance

Stock balance helps users know whether a product can be committed to an order.

Use stock adjustments when:

- initial stock is added
- stock is corrected after a manual count
- inventory needs to reflect a business correction

## Using inventory in orders

When creating an order, users can select products from Inventory instead of manually typing every line.

This helps keep order lines consistent with the product catalogue.

## Tips

- Add or correct products before creating orders.
- Keep sale price and tax rate current.
- Review low or unusual stock before confirming orders.
- Use manual lines only when a product does not belong in the catalogue yet.
