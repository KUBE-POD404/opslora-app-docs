# Settings

Settings control organization-level details that affect invoices, tax, portal behavior, feature availability, and Lora AI consent.

## Business profile

Use the business profile to keep organization identity and billing information accurate.

Review this before sending invoices or sharing customer-facing documents.

## Tax profile

Use the tax profile for tax registration and tax-related details that may appear on invoices.

Keep this up to date when legal or billing details change.

## Invoice defaults

Invoice defaults reduce repetitive work when creating invoices.

Review defaults for:

- due date behavior
- numbering or display preferences where available
- default notes or terms where available

## Invoice templates

Invoice templates control how invoice documents appear to customers.

Use templates to keep customer-facing documents consistent with the business identity.

## Feature flags

Feature flags expose app capabilities that may be enabled, disabled, or staged.

Only change feature flags when you understand the expected user impact.

## Portal settings

Portal settings are for customer-facing access and presentation options where available.

Use them to control how customers interact with published or shared experiences.

## Lora AI consent

Lora AI consent controls whether Opslora can share application data with the assistant.

Use it to:

- enable or disable Lora AI data sharing
- review consent metadata
- choose provider/model options where available
- manage privacy and retention expectations

## Profile settings

Profile settings are for the signed-in user's account and preference information.
