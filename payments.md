# Payments

Payments show money collected against invoices and help users understand what remains unpaid.

## What you can do

- View payment records.
- Search payment activity where available.
- Open payment transaction details.
- Add a payment from an invoice payment flow.
- Refund a payment where available.

## Add a payment

1. Open an unpaid or partially paid invoice.
2. Choose Pay or Add payment.
3. Enter the payment amount.
4. Select the payment method.
5. Submit the payment.
6. Return to the invoice detail to confirm the new balance/status.

## Refund a payment

Use refunds carefully. A refund changes the financial state and may update the related invoice status.

Before refunding:

- confirm the correct invoice
- confirm the correct payment
- confirm the refund reason and amount

## Payment review checklist

- Are there unpaid invoices with customer follow-up needed?
- Are there partially paid invoices that need a second collection?
- Are there refunds that need operational review?
- Does invoice status match payment history?
