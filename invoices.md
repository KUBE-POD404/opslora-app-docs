# Invoices

Invoices turn confirmed customer work into a billable record. The invoice area helps users see what is open, what has been paid, and what needs action.

## What you can do

- View invoice list.
- Filter by status where available.
- Open invoice detail.
- Create payment flows from invoice detail or invoice list.
- Cancel unpaid invoices where allowed.
- Refund paid invoices where available.
- Download invoice documents where available.

## Create an invoice

Invoices are normally created from confirmed orders.

1. Open Orders.
2. Select a confirmed order.
3. Choose Create invoice.
4. Review invoice template/defaults.
5. Create the invoice.

## Invoice statuses

Common status meanings:

| Status | Meaning |
| --- | --- |
| Unpaid/Open | Payment is still due. |
| Partially paid | Some money has been recorded, but the invoice is not fully paid. |
| Paid | Payment has been completed. |
| Refunded | A refund action has been recorded. |
| Cancelled | The invoice should no longer be collected. |

## Invoice detail

Use invoice detail to review:

- customer and order context
- invoice lines and totals
- due date and status
- payment history
- action buttons for payment or document download

## Best practices

- Create invoices only after confirming the order.
- Check customer and tax details before invoice creation.
- Use invoice defaults and templates to reduce manual correction.
- Review open invoices regularly.
