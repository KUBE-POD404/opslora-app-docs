# Orders

Orders capture what a customer wants to buy. They connect customer records, product lines, confirmation decisions, and invoice creation.

## What you can do

- View orders.
- Filter orders by status or customer where available.
- Create a new order.
- Edit an order while it is still in progress.
- Confirm an order.
- Cancel an order.
- Create an invoice from a confirmed order.

## Create an order

1. Open Orders.
2. Choose Create order.
3. Select the customer.
4. Add product-backed or manual order lines.
5. Review quantities, prices, and totals.
6. Save the order.

## Confirm an order

Confirm an order when it is ready to move forward.

A confirmed order is the normal starting point for invoice creation.

Before confirming:

- check the customer
- check order lines
- check quantities
- check product and stock assumptions

## Create an invoice from an order

1. Open Orders.
2. Find a confirmed order without an invoice.
3. Choose the create-invoice action.
4. Review the invoice preview.
5. Confirm invoice creation.

## Cancel an order

Cancel an order when it should no longer move toward invoicing.

Use cancellation rather than editing history when the business decision is that the order should stop.

## Operational signals

Orders to watch:

- draft orders waiting for confirmation
- confirmed orders without invoices
- cancelled orders that may need customer follow-up
- orders with product or stock issues
